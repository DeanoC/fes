# i386 small forms: XCHG, MOV moffs, XLAT, SALC, CALL, PUSH and POP of r/m

Dated record, 2026-10-27. Host simulation and routed diagnostics on Powerboat.
No package, RBF or kit.

After [exception delivery](2026-10-27-i386-exceptions.md), the CPU gains the
remaining data-movement forms that reuse its existing datapaths:
- XCHG (86h, 87h, 91h-97h);
- MOV between AL/eAX and a direct offset (A0h-A3h);
- XLAT and SALC;
- CALL r/m near (FF /2), PUSH r/m (FF /6) and POP r/m (8Fh).

POP m with 32-bit addressing is not built. The BCD adjusts, the bit tests
and BSF/BSR need new EX logic and come in a later PR under the same issue.
ENTER waits for the string slice's looping, and BOUND for MUL/DIV.

Run data: [`2026-10-27-i386-forms.jsonl`](2026-10-27-i386-forms.jsonl).
- The before figures are exception delivery's `a18ad37ac5` (its record's
  rows).
- Routes and the full lockstep runs are of `d073eaa778`; `a5744f2f34` after
  it adds one directed case.
- A Quartus row's `fingerprint` is the first 12 hex digits of the SHA-256 of
  its `sources` map, as JSON with sorted keys.

## The forms

| Instruction | Uops |
| --- | --- |
| XCHG r, r | three XORs that write no flags, each into the register the one before read (SEQ_XCHG); a MOV when both name one register |
| XCHG r/m, r with memory | the operand into T1; the register stored to it; the register from T1 (SEQ_XCHGM); LOCK may prefix it |
| MOV AL/eAX, moffs and back | one uop, the offset after the opcode by address size, with no base or index |
| XLAT | one load from [BX + AL] or [EBX + AL] |
| SALC | SBB AL, AL, writing no flags |
| CALL r/m near | the target, zero-extended by operand size, into T1; the push of the next EIP, which checks T1 against CS's limit first; the jump to T1 (SEQ_CALLI) |
| PUSH m | the operand into T1; its push (SEQ_PUSHM) |
| POP m | the stack's slot into T1, ESP unmoved; T1 stored to the operand; a pop into nothing, which steps ESP (SEQ_POPM) |

PUSH and POP of a register r/m are PUSH r and POP r.

- **Flags follow the uop.** EX's flag selects for arithmetic and logic ops
  now take the uop's flag-write bits, decided in M2 as before, so XCHG's
  XORs and SALC's SBB write none. A first run without this changed EFLAGS
  on 191 XCHG and 98 SALC states.
- **Direct operands.** The decode's `direct` covers a moffs and the vector's
  entry: no base or index, and no BP-based SS default from whatever byte 1
  looks like as a ModRM.
- **XLAT's index** is AL zero-extended. D's address forwarding masks the
  index to its low byte (`index8`), beside the 16-bit mask it already had,
  and holds the uop when AL would arrive late, since a late index enters AG
  whole.
- **POP m keeps its operand's address.** Its first uop is a stack read, so
  dc's address fields describe the stack slot; DC keeps the ModRM operand's
  registers and segment beside them (`m_*`), and the store takes them back.
- **The sequence kind** widens to 4 bits for the five new sequences, and
  `b_t1` (a uop's src2 from T1) returns for the moves through T1.

## What the 386EX does

- **A faulting POP m leaves ESP as it was.** No corpus POP m that faults
  moves ESP, including the 7 whose store to the operand raises #GP. So the store
  comes before ESP's step.
- **A 32-bit CALL r/m checks its target before it pushes.** The corpus has no
  32-bit CALL r/m; a directed case (CALL EBX to 10000h) holds the RTL to
  Bochs, which raises #GP(0) with nothing pushed, as for CALL rel.

## Result

| | Before (`a18ad37ac5`) | After (`d073eaa778`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nextpnr `bd7a1c0d` (HIP, gpu-run) | 70.32, 68.82, 71.14, 59.96 (DC ×4) | 68.62, 68.98, 73.39, 69.66 (DC, DC, EX, Q) |
| Quartus 17.0.2 (oracle) | 96.20, 99.64, 99.54, 101.56 (Q ×4) | 105.04, 103.90, 99.28, 100.39 (Q ×4) |

**nextpnr averages 70.2 MHz against 67.6**, which was low mostly on its seed
4. Each seed sits within the delivery slice's range.
- Seed 1 is D's issue into `dc` again (8 cells, 11.0 ns of routing).
- Seeds 2 and 4 run from the data port's valid pin through `freeze` and
  `exc_take` into the redirect, which restarts DC and Q. That path came with
  exception delivery; this slice does not touch it.
- Seed 3 is EX's flags (10 cells, 5 of them carry).

**Quartus averages 102.2 MHz against 99.2,** Q-limited on every seed.

**Yosys's depth:** D 8, DC 11, EX 11, M1 7, M2 8, WB 7, against 10, 11, 11,
7, 8, 6. XLAT's byte mask sits in D's address forwarding beside the 16-bit
one and adds no level; the decode's new cases leave DC at 11.

## Cycle costs

Without latency, measured on corpus states as cycles from start to HLT,
against a NOP's 17.

| Instruction | Extra cycles | 386 clocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XCHG r, r | 2 | 3 |
| XCHG r/m, r with memory | about 10 | 5 |
| MOV moffs, XLAT, SALC | 0 | 4 (load) or 2, 5, - |
| CALL r/m near | 17 | 7 (register), 10 (memory) |
| PUSH m | 6 | 5 |
| POP m | 8 | 5 |

- The moves through T1 wait for T1's load to retire, about five cycles.
- CALL r/m pays that wait and a taken branch's redirect; CALL rel costs 11.

**Corpus.** 49,500 new states in 39 files: the 18 stems with their 66h and
67h forms. 678Fh and 67668Fh, POP m with 32-bit addressing, stay out.

**Random programs** add:
- XCHG of registers and with memory, and XCHG eAX, r;
- MOV with a direct offset, XLAT and SALC, 16-bit addressed;
- PUSH and POP r/m beside the other pushes and pops (no memory POP in a
  subroutine's body, which must leave its return address alone);
- CALL r/m near in a quarter of the call blocks, through a register a MOV
  loads or a word or dword one stores just before. The target is an EIP, so
  these stop once a far transfer has moved CS.

**Holds.** XCHG of registers, three uops, gets its own gap bucket. A store
counts for the store holds only as an instruction's last uop, which XCHG's
with memory and POP m's are not.

Mutations, each on the whole corpus and 2 × 200 random programs, of
`d073eaa778`:

| --- | --- |
| EX's flag selects ignore the uop's flag-write bits | 2,621 corpus states and 308 of 400 programs fail |
| XLAT's index not cut to its byte | 4,314 corpus states and 1 of 400 programs fail |
| CALL r/m pushes before it checks its target | nothing failed on `d073eaa778`; the directed CALL r/m case added after it fails |

## What the design doc should say

This is reported for the doc's owner to write; the doc is unchanged here.

- **XCHG of registers** is three uops of the early ALU, no temporaries.
- **The moves through T1** (XCHG with memory, CALL, PUSH and POP of r/m) are
  fixed sequences; each waits for T1's load to retire. Forwarding T1 like a
  GPR would remove the wait.
- **POP m** stores before it steps ESP, as the 386EX's restartable fault
  needs.
- **Not built:** POP m with 32-bit addressing, whose ESP base reads ESP after
  the pop.
- **Next:** the BCD adjusts, the bit tests and BSF/BSR in EX; then MUL and
  DIV, strings, I/O and external interrupts.
