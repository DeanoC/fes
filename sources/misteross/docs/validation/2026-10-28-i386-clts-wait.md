# i386 CLTS and WAIT

Dated record, 2026-10-28. Host simulation and routed diagnostics on Powerboat.
No package, RBF or kit.

This slice builds CLTS (`0F 06`) and WAIT (`9B`), as the design's
[FPU](../i386-cpu-design.md#fpu) section asks: WAIT raises #NM when CR0.MP
and CR0.TS are both set and otherwise does nothing; CLTS clears CR0.TS. It
is stacked on [the BOUND slice](2026-10-28-i386-bound.md).

## What the corpus can say

Every SingleStepTests state, the 2,600 of `0F06` and `9B` among them, starts
and ends with CR0 = 7FFEFFF0h: MP and TS clear. So the corpus checks only
that CLTS and WAIT change nothing else, and that LOCK on either is #UD (3
states of `0F06`). The rules themselves, #NM only with MP and TS both set
and TS cleared by CLTS, are the 386's architected ones (the design cites the
386 manual, §11.1.5.1), which Bochs implements; random programs check them
against Bochs from MP and TS at random.

## How it runs

- **CR0.** The RTL held none of CR0. It now holds MP and TS, all these two
  instructions need: reset loads them from `init_cr0`, `arch_cr0` shows
  them, and nothing but CLTS writes them. MOV to CR0, LMSW and the ESC
  instructions, the other readers and writers, are not built.
- **WAIT** is a NOP, or `FAULT_NM` (vector 7) when MP and TS are both set,
  which DC decodes from the two registers after every other check (#UD for
  LOCK, #GP(0) past 15 bytes). Nothing is added to D's issue path or the
  freeze.
- **CLTS** is a system uop that clears TS at WB and jumps to the next
  instruction, through the taken-branch path a JMP takes. So every
  instruction after it decodes once TS is clear, and nothing waits in D
  behind it. The jump costs a redirect, which only CLTS pays. It is not cut
  to 16 bits: a CLTS that ends at FFFFh leaves EIP 10000h, so the next fetch
  raises #GP(0), as after any other instruction.

## The testbench

- `SCOPE` gains `0F06` and `9B` (2,600 states).
- The lockstep compares CR0's MP and TS at every retirement and with the
  corpus's final state.
- Random programs start with MP and TS at random, and gain CLTS, WAIT, and
  CLTS then WAIT, now and then with a prefix. MP and TS are both set in a
  quarter of the programs, where a WAIT that no CLTS precedes raises #NM.
  CLTS counts as a taken branch in the gap statistics.

**Depth.** Synthesized as `scripts/benchmark_fes_i386.py` does, before any
route, D (11 LUT levels), DC (12) and EX (12 and one carry cell) are as at
the base: WAIT's #NM adds two registers to DC's decode, off its deepest
path. Two stages this slice does not touch moved: Q's deepest path, from
its aligner's start to its own output register, from 9 to 10 levels, and
WB's, from `md_busy` to `nmi_pend`, from 7 to 9; L2 went from 11 to 10. ABC
maps the whole netlist at once, so a change anywhere moves such paths, as
the bit test record found for DC.
