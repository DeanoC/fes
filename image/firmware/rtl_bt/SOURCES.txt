The kernel requests these names for the Superstation One RTL8821CU
(rtl8821c) and for common Realtek USB dongles (rtl8761bu):

  rtl_bt/rtl8821c_fw.bin
  rtl_bt/rtl8821c_config.bin
  rtl_bt/rtl8761bu_fw.bin
  rtl_bt/rtl8761bu_config.bin

Redistribution in binary form follows LICENCE.rtlwifi_firmware.txt.
SHA256SUMS is the pin checked when the image is assembled.
This note stays in the repository and is not copied into the rootfs.
