# USB recovery and subsequent warm preparation stop

The requested host-side reset followed by volatile JTAG recovery restored the top kit without another physical power cycle after the original USB Close panic. This fresh candidate boot subsequently detected the USB boot keyboard. The host reset alone did not establish runtime recovery, and the JTAG operation reloaded the FPGA/HPS recovery image rather than toggling only USB0.

Independent raw replay verifies the initial MENU transfer of 1,708,032 bytes, two posts/two acknowledgements, retirement and continuation. The initial transfer took 11,548,828 microseconds.

The first retained source released successfully in 929,950 microseconds, generation 4 to 10. The next warm source stopped at preparation stage 2 with status -110 before command-queue submission or further configuration DATA. The last accepted timestamp is 998,532 microseconds after preparation start, with 5,120 loads and 2,816 stores. The previous source reached 1,792 stores at this boundary; the expected-word repair improves progress but does not fit the current one-second preparation limit. This is a preparation failure, not evidence of another USB failure. The final USB Close repair was not reached and remains physically unqualified.

The frozen procedure stopped once and was not retried. Candidate FIT remains on the spare card; original FIT is retained on the host, not restored. All ten attempt process records are independently stopped, the root UART FD scan is clear, and both role cleanups restore auto/15 MHz JTAG and exact USB identity/inode/permissions. The broader root scan also covers preflight process records. The claim remains held. QSPI, factory card and bottom kit are untouched.

The earlier USB-only recovery probe explicitly excludes this private source/SMMU failure class. It cannot authorize recovery or DMA/source reuse from the new stopped state. The consumed procedure records a physical reset requirement before restoration; no new physical reset, USB reset, JTAG reload or trial retry was performed during evidence collection.
