### C64 and PC/XT: owed

Review of #2177 found these left out, and [method.md](method.md#added-on-24-nov-2026-after-review)
adds them. Their D12 has not run. d12-slat.sh has their rows; at this
branch's merge base the run is:

    d12-slat.sh 837520f8f ARMREV c64pal c64nativepal pcxt

This record must then give, for each:
- the inputs comparison, A against B, by [d12_cmp_id.py](d12_cmp_id.py);
- C's `core.rbf` against A's. For the PC/XT this follows method.md's rule
  from #2182: if C differs, A is repeated;
- B's gates.

Until that is recorded here, or Deano scopes them out, this branch does not
show D12 for the C64 or the PC/XT. The native-shell models have not been
through this harness before: #2036's D12 left out C64 `nativepal`.

### Not covered

Spectrum `plus3` (`fes.spectrum-plus3`), `pentagon` (`fes.pentagon`) and
`native48` hold `machine/board.py` too. Neither method.md nor #2036's D12
covers them, and the review did not name them. The support matrix lists
`fes.spectrum-plus3` and `fes.pentagon` as full-check cores. Whether they take
D12 here is Deano's call; d12-slat.sh has their rows.
