## HDMI and audio

Capture used ASUS 4KPRO serial `802B003090700329` through its stable by-id
video path: YUYV422, 1280×720 at requested 60 fps, with no scaling. Each load
has a 180-frame lossless FFV1 recording and a frame-120 PNG. ALSA
`hw:CARD=D4KPRO,DEV=0` supplied six seconds of 48 kHz stereo signed-16 PCM.
The full recordings and operator logs remain under the task worktree's ignored
`out/hardware/video-parts-kit2-20261002/`; their hashes are retained in the
result receipt. Published FLAC clips preserve the captured PCM losslessly.

The [direct frame](video-parts-kit2-2026-10-02/direct.png) and
[scanline frame](video-parts-kit2-2026-10-02/scanlines.png) have identical
image bounds: x384–895, y168–551. Corresponding SGM PNGs are byte-identical
to these video-only captures. Captured even-row RGB pixels within the image
agree exactly between the direct and scanline selections. Odd/even ratios
are 0.4933 for the green border's green channel and 0.4931 for a red square's
red channel. Mean absolute error from the odd-row half-brightness model is
under 1.02 capture RGB units per channel.

Independent [capture analysis](video-parts-kit2-2026-10-02/capture-analysis.json)
checks checkerboard cell centers and gaps, picture bounds, row parity and
the distinct decoded frame variants. Each recording starts with two or three
black acquisition frames; the remaining 177–178 retain the expected picture,
with no later bad frames. Decoded capture timestamps are monotonic, with one
50 ms acquisition gap per recording; they do not directly measure FPGA HS/VS.
Capture conversion introduces variation of at most one RGB unit from each
recording's PNG, so relaunch establishes the same geometry and pattern rather
than equality of every PNG byte.

Both [direct + SGM](video-parts-kit2-2026-10-02/direct-sgm.flac) and
[scanlines + SGM](video-parts-kit2-2026-10-02/scanlines-sgm.flac) contain
the expected SN tone near 216.784 Hz and AY tone near 440.397 Hz, above the
[quiet graphics baseline](video-parts-kit2-2026-10-02/quiet.flac). The analysis
requires frequency error no greater than 0.5 Hz and fundamental amplitude
above both 100 signed-16 capture units and twenty times the quiet baseline.
The 4KPRO converts color/range and filters audio: these observations establish
the intended picture and retained tones, not bit-exact FPGA RGB/PCM levels.
