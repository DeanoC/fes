Evidence class
These are zero-BUILD_ID frozen-netlist RTL experiments, not production
qualification. Each netlist comes from the ST producer's own functions:
build_commands (its Yosys program), prepare_cpu_inputs, clock_read_only_memories
and validate_synth_evidence. BUILD_ID is 0, the producer's --synth-only value.
socket_qsf() supplies the QSF, and the SDC is the producer's clocks-oss.sdc.
Routes use the producer's nextpnr argv: weight 2000, critexp 5, --router gpu,
search-mode --timing-allow-fail, and the 1800 s nextpnr_lease.py lease timeout.
They also use its environment (HOME, LANG=C, LC_ALL=C, TZ=UTC, slot PATH), with
one gpu-run lease per route (owner lander, 1 GPU). The production QoR search
additionally runs nextpnr under the strace read-audit; these routes do not.
A run passes when every clock's final analogue signoff fmax meets its
constraint: pixel_clk 74.25, system_clock.clocks[0] 52.22 and clocks[1] 12.29 MHz.
The baseline netlist reproduces #913's CUDA no-DSP table exactly at seed 4
(route timeout) and seed 5 (64.92 / 54.85 MHz), on HIP.
No producer seal was run. The current host's ST toolchain key resolves to slot
6e2837613d66767ee9d1bad984d4dc3415487603b14b11ea6c166eaea0e00adb, which is not
built. The producer bootstraps it on its next run.

Critical paths at 811a5a34 (#913's production search, 7 of 10 seeds routed)
pixel_clk failed on all 7 routed seeds, at 64.24-71.74 MHz:
- Border consume: event M10K output head0..2 (4.5-6.4 ns of route), then the
  held/head select, the consume-bank select and the x compare, into
  raster_rgb_q, remaining, colour, next_pointer and held_valid. Worst slack
  -0.471 (s5) to -2.098 ns (s10).
- Border event address: descriptor M10K output desc0..2, through the
  read-pointer LUT4, to the B1ADDR of all 18 8Kx1 event M10Ks in the bank.
  Worst -1.029 ns (s2).
- Frame read: the 16:1 group selects sit next to the frame M10K columns. In one
  cycle, read_chunk's index travels out to them and the selected word travels
  back to group_read. Worst -0.927 ns (s2).
sys[0] failed on 2 of 7 (s4 50.18, s2 51.74 MHz). The path runs from the ROM lane
M10Ks through the 192-lane OR to upper_byte/rdata. On s3 it had 10.8 ns of route.
Every one of these paths starts at an M10K output; the build uses 513 of 553
M10Ks.

Analogue repair (nextpnr 9128800f)
At 811a5a34, final signoff was worse than the first analogue check on 6 of 7
routed seeds. Examples: s3 74.12 -> 69.77, s4 73.00 -> 68.74, s10 70.60 -> 64.24
MHz. For s3, nextpnr restored the routing with its best recorded slack
(-0.024 ns, the first check), but final signoff measured -0.865 ns. Signoff
re-times arcs that the analogue model cannot time with the calibration
accumulated over the repair rounds (analogue.cc 551-556). The repair also runs
on a 300 s wall-clock budget.

Routability
Final SA placement wirelength predicts routing within the 1800 s budget, across
every netlist here. Every seed below 368k routed. Above 376k, every seed but one
timed out; the exception, bf1281a0 s2, routed at 388k. Between those, 811a5a34 s8
timed out at 368.5k, while its s4/s6 routed at 370-372k. The RTL changes do not
target routability.

Changes and equivalence (sims.txt)
bf1281a0, in st_native_border:
- One held register loads the consume bank's prefetched head whenever
  held_valid is clear. The consume bank changes only at SOF, which also clears
  held_valid.
- consume_current requires held_valid and compares only the held event's x.
- Each descriptor RAM's read data takes one more pixel edge into desc0..2.
- The adapter sets output_row 6 edges before line start and 11 before SOF.
  Line start and SOF fall in blanking (border_sample_ce = 0). Each native pixel
  is held for two output_ce edges.
- A row's event x values strictly increase, so no event is due on the edge after
  a consume. A probe in the original RTL confirms that the real adapter path
  never consumes on such an edge.
- native_border_tb now drives the adapter's timing: one blanking edge after
  SOF/line start, and two output_ce edges per native pixel in the full-arena
  replay, checked on both edges.
b72c1756, in st_native_low_video:
- Each bank's 16-chunk group registers its own hit with the RAM read, and loads
  its own chunk index only when it is the addressed group. The distinct enables
  keep Yosys from merging the fifteen index registers.
- A miss, a chunk past the frame and reset clear the group's selected word, and
  the RGB output is the OR of the fifteen group words.
- RAM ports, read latency and the SOF in-flight bank tag are unchanged.
Every video sim result line is identical to main's: native-video, video-adapter,
shifter-output, short-line-video and cache-write-coherence. The only difference
is native_border_tb's check count.
