EXEC captures the ALU, each independent address/PC result, branch and alignment
flags, store payload and CSR operand. COMMIT applies instruction side effects
from those stable values. This adds one execute cycle per instruction: with
a one-cycle memory, ALU/branch/jump instructions take four clocks and loads/
stores six. The bus, ISA and shared 74.25 MHz clock are unchanged. Only
fes.riscv consumes this CPU in the current tree. No shared ABI or factory change.
