## Machine-observed checks

source per launch, validates shared session/generation/token identity and
distinct routing/SSRCs, starts video before audio, rolls back video on audio
failure, and independently bounds cleanup. The bridge tests authenticate
`MEDIA_HELLO`, reject a wrong token, consume non-zero PCM16 RTP, retain a

## Operator observations and inference

Genki can render and play the active Sonic run, including while its own
application is authorized. That observation must be kept separate from raw
ShadowCast endpoint capture. If FogCast remains exact-zero while Genki is
audible, the strongest host hypothesis is an application-rendered or
exclusive-owner path (or a route/permission mismatch), not a silent MiSTer
source. The next experiment is a named/indexed CoreAudio comparison while
viewer and helper ownership are tested alternately and concurrently, followed
by a known sound-capable HDMI sink or independent analyzer if target playback
is required.
