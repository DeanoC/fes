# DE25-Nano MAC0 observation before storage

This diagnostic samples MAC0's clock, reset, firewall and stream-ID configuration from the CBL's initial EL3 SPL. Use the RX-only UART verifier to interpret the two complete register samples. A reported `tsn0-reset` means that the SPL observed an asserted MAC or ECC reset and skipped the MAC version read. A changing sample rejects the capture. The system-manager NoC idle word describes HPS bridges and supplies no MAC DMA-drain evidence. The result does not grant the native network driver DMA access.

The builder starts with the vendor carrier and the original CBL initial-trace producer at `d3ec4a3928eac87477b67d7c92d6e63c4a0d1bc7`. It retains the qualified clock/console initialization, startup object checks and unconditional OCRAM Park before DDR, SD, QSPI or BL31. Its additional MMIO operations are normal-value register reads from the Agilex 5 CS/R1 register map. MAC0's CSR interface uses L4 SP. Reading `MAC_Version` also requires stable clock sources and deasserted MAC0 and ECC resets. [Altera register map](https://docs.altera.com/v/u/resources/864455/agilex-5-cs-and-r1-suffix-opn-hps-register-map) · [XGMAC clocks](https://docs.altera.com/r/docs/814346/26.1.1/hard-processor-system-technical-reference-manual-agilex-5-socs/xgmac-clocks?contentId=ZVIw1NaUa2BuEkJDgdUxAA)

Build in a private checkout on a filesystem with at least 30 GiB free. Pass `build.py --trace-checkout <original-trace-checkout>` the vendor U-Boot checkout, cold serial seed, matching entry, compiler, vendor SOF, Quartus PFG and fresh output arguments from the historical producer. The output must be under the historical checkout's `out/`. The builder checks the emitted OCRAM bytes inside the candidate SOF and compares the unchanged vendor FPGA sections. The resulting `mac-observation.json` identifies the candidate, its UART ID and the exact parser. [Qualified original trace](../../../../docs/validation/2026-11-01-de25-initial-trace/README.md)

On an assigned CBL, the diagnostic uses the volatile JTAG SOF operation and the board's if02 receive-only UART. The operator arms UART before dispatch, checks the original SPL build banner and 18 checkpoints, replays both register samples through `verify.verify(raw, diagnostic_id)` and requires the final hardware Park. Restore the baseline named in the current CBL ledger using its qualified volatile procedure and verify its boot and Park before releasing the claim.

## MAC0 after DDR calibration

`build_admission.py` packages a separate volatile SPL from the [CBL-qualified v2 CPU parking source](../../../../docs/validation/2026-11-01-de25-initial-parking-v2/README.md). Supply its checkout at `87b2d7f4721c819b554212f2b80c193a07a2531e` with `--trace-checkout`, its matched cold seed and entry, and the historical producer's vendor U-Boot, cross-compiler, SOF, PFG and fresh output arguments. The SPL executes the stock security and DDR initialization, reports two eligibility samples, then Parks before storage or BL31. The build checks the linked symbols and compares every embedded SPL byte with the ARM build. It excludes the transmitter, SMMU configuration routines, frame and descriptor writer.

The samples report the boot CPU and power ownership, primary RAM bank and global RAM size, L4 and EMAC clock sources and rates, TSN0 reset and stream routing, and the nonsecure and secure SMMU controls. The SDM `CONFIG_STATUS` query uses the vendor mailbox, which writes a status request to the mailbox FIFO and consumes its reply. Each pass records the transport result, returned length and six response words alongside the vendor's interpretation. A successful transport can report a fabric that has reached `CONF_DONE` but still lacks `INIT_DONE`. An unavailable supplier or moving configuration remains visible in the report. [CBL phase-one SDM evidence](../../../../docs/validation/2026-11-01-de25-fpga-ready/README.md)

`verify_admission.decode_admission(raw, receipt)` replays the 32 initial and terminal CPU checkpoints, both register samples and Park. `held` requires a stable reset-held MAC/ECC, the original stream route, idle disabled SMMU controls and verified CPU ownership. `eligible` additionally evaluates the prerequisites used by the transmitter. An observed CSR clock from 100 MHz up to 150 MHz uses the existing MDIO divider of 62. Higher documented clock ranges use different divider settings. The report reproduces the original TX04 startup predicates, including its 125 MHz parent assumption and its requirement that the SPL already masked SError. The later transmitter establishes its own exception mask and uses the documented parent clock. ## Single-frame TX diagnostic

`build_positive.py` uses the same v2 boot inputs and takes `--frame` and `--frame-sha256`. The 60-byte private frame includes the witness's unicast destination, the assigned CBL source, EtherType `0x88b5`, `FES-CBL-TX-v1` followed by NUL, a 16-byte per-run nonce and 16 zero pad bytes. The destination and nonce are part of the complete digest supplied to the producer. Use a fresh witness and capture on the assigned receiver to qualify transmission.

The fitted design selects EMACA as the EMAC0 reference and routes RGMII with MDIO through the dedicated HPS pins. The SPL checks the EMAC0 selector, PLL lock, counter gates, bypass and reset, and uses the vendor clock provider to verify the 250 MHz controller reference and 100 MHz CSR clock. While the MAC and ECC are in reset it selects RGMII (1). It reads back TX_CONFIG.SS=011 before enabling TX. At 1 Gb/s Altera specifies that the hard MAC divides the reference by two to supply the 125 MHz RGMII TX clock. This is a derived clock rate. Clause 22 checks the PHY identity, both link partners’ advertisements, and the final 1000BASE-T full-duplex status reported in PHY register 31. [Microchip KSZ9031RNX Table 4-4](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/UNG/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/KSZ9031RNX-Data-Sheet-DS00002117.pdf). [Altera XGMAC clocks](https://docs.altera.com/r/docs/814346/26.1.1/hard-processor-system-technical-reference-manual-agilex-5-socs/xgmac-clocks) and [EMAC HPS initialization](https://docs.altera.com/r/docs/814346/26.1.1/hard-processor-system-technical-reference-manual-agilex-5-socs/emac-hps-interface-initialization)

After the startup suppliers pass, the verified EL3 primary checks that debug, IRQ and FIQ are masked and that ISR_EL1.A is clear. It sets PSTATE.A with `MSR DAIFSet,#4`, reads back all four masks, and checks the pending SError bit during the trial. The terminal SPL retains these local masks through Park. [Arm exception model](https://developer.arm.com/documentation/102412/latest)

After releasing MAC0 from reset, the SPL records the full version, all three hardware-feature registers and the current system-manager interface selector before testing them. HW_FEATURE0.PHYSEL reflects the selected pins. With RGMII active it reads 1, while the register map lists a reset value of 0. The diagnostic requires the fitted XGMAC revision, RGMII and gigabit support, MDIO, enhanced AXI DMA, 40-bit address capability, 32 KiB TX and 16 KiB RX FIFOs, and eight RX/TX queues and channels. The MAC can advertise unused options such as timestamping, offload and wake features independently of this transfer. The stock Linux device tree specifies RGMII and the same FIFO and queue configuration. [Altera register map](https://docs.altera.com/v/u/resources/864455/agilex-5-cs-and-r1-suffix-opn-hps-register-map), [Terasic DE25 device tree](https://github.com/terasic/linux-socfpga/blob/d7d192a9ddd955fe206aa350afed8f82e368a74c/arch/arm64/boot/dts/intel/socfpga_agilex5_de25_nano.dts#L47-L58) and [Agilex 5 queue configuration](https://github.com/terasic/linux-socfpga/blob/d7d192a9ddd955fe206aa350afed8f82e368a74c/arch/arm64/boot/dts/intel/socfpga_agilex5.dtsi#L318-L390).

The standalone SPL keeps MAC0 in reset while it creates a default-abort stream table and a two-page nonidentity stage-2 mapping for SID 1. One mapped page holds the descriptors, and the second permits only reads of the frame. The physical backing at the two IOVA values is checked empty. The SPL checks its CPU, stream, translation-table and queue ownership at each DMA boundary. A completed TX requires descriptor completion, a fresh process-stopped acknowledgement, an idle MAC queue, acknowledged translation revocation, matching reset readback and Park. `verify_positive.decode_positive(raw, receipt)` replays the target half. The receiver must independently observe the entire matching frame. `decode_closed` also recognizes a proven, untouched refusal or a revoked mapping before any DMA became possible. Uncertain domain state requires the recovery procedure for that board.


The fitted KSZ9031RNX is at MDIO address 1 on the plain RGMII interface. Once its ID and normal gigabit advertisement match, the SPL saves the Clause 22 MMD selector, address pointer and both fast-link-pulse timing registers. Microchip documents two valid register pairs. The default 8 ms burst interval can cause intermittent negotiation with some partners. The SPL applies the specified 16 ms interval at MMD 0 registers 4 and 3, verifies both words, and issues one auto-negotiation restart while preserving the programmed BMCR and advertisements. A PHY already set to 16 ms needs only the restart. The earlier CBL traces contain no timing-register observation. [Microchip KSZ9031RNX tables 4-5 and 4-6 and Figure 7-3](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/UNG/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/KSZ9031RNX-Data-Sheet-DS00002117.pdf), [Terasic Linux driver](https://github.com/terasic/linux-socfpga/blob/d7d192a9ddd955fe206aa350afed8f82e368a74c/drivers/net/phy/micrel.c#L1074-L1090) and [Terasic U-Boot driver](https://github.com/terasic/u-boot-socfpga/blob/d0f8813fd6bf8191d49c8b8d2035ffd2ae10c413/drivers/net/phy/micrel_ksz90x1.c#L203-L219).

The firmware samples Clause 22 status before the first write, after the restart, at roughly five-second intervals and for the final qualifying pair. Within 25 seconds it requires two samples at least 100 ms apart showing current link and completed auto-negotiation, compatible gigabit advertisements, both 1000BASE-T receivers ready, the gigabit PMA/PCS link and resolved 1000 full duplex. A saturated idle-error sample breaks the pair. Register 1F bit 0 is recorded independently on both samples. The fitted board's Linux console previously reported 4.09 seconds between RGMII configuration and gigabit link-up. [Factory CBL wire log](../../../../docs/validation/2026-10-31-de25-owner-binding/bus-boot-serial.log)

Each 40-second grant reserves two seconds for TX completion and eight seconds for cleanup. The bidirectional grant also budgets two seconds for RX. After a verified channel stop or an undispatched refusal, the SPL restores changed timing, restarts negotiation with that original pair, restores the MMD portal and reads back the original timing, selector, BMCR and advertisements before resetting the controller. A PHY restoration that cannot be verified requires cold recovery. The operator also checks the independent DMA retirement and baseline boot evidence.

If a dispatched descriptor fails or times out under the still-owned domain, the SPL issues the normal channel stop once and requires a fresh process-stopped acknowledgement. It disables transmission and samples the stopped channel FSMs, idle AXI master and empty MTL twice before revoking translation and resetting the controller. A completed recovery is reported as `failed-drained`. Only `closed` qualifies a successful target TX.


## Two grants in one boot

Pass a second private request to `build_positive.py` with `--second-frame` and `--second-frame-sha256` to build the distinct bidirectional diagnostic. The two frames must have the same endpoints and different 128-bit nonces. Each witness reply reverses the MAC addresses and changes the marker to `FES-CBL-RX-v1`. The build emits the request and expected reply hashes in `mac-bidirectional.json`.

The receiver adds one nonidentity, device-write-only page and places its 32 descriptors in the unused half of the existing descriptor page. Only one receive descriptor is posted. After each exchange the SPL stops TX, closes ingress, stops RX, verifies both channel FSMs and the shared AXI master idle, revokes SID1, and reads back the hard reset. It monitors the retired buffers and abort mapping over at least 10 ms. The second grant requires the exact retired bytes and a complete stream and stage-2 table comparison. With MAC0 held in reset it breaks the abort STE, synchronizes invalidation, invalidates the new VMID, rebuilds the buffers and publishes a translated STE for VMID2. The SMMU stays enabled across the transition. Each grant requires a fresh matching reply. A failed first grant leaves the second unopened.
